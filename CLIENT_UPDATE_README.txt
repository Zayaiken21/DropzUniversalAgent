Dropz Universal Agent — Client Update Instructions

1. Download DropzUniversalAgent-Windows.zip.
2. Right-click the ZIP and choose Extract All.
3. Open DropzUniversalAgent.exe.
4. The app opens in your browser at 127.0.0.1.
5. Enter your license token.
6. Save your MT5 Demo/Live credentials in Settings.
7. Open TradeSmart and connect.

Automatic updates:
- When a new version is available, the app downloads and installs it automatically.
- Keep the app folder together. Do not remove DropzUpdater.exe.
- If Windows shows SmartScreen, click More Info → Run Anyway.
